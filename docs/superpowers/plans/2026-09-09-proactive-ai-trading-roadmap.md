# Proactive AI Trading Roadmap

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-09-proactive-ai-trading-design.md`

Execute these plans in order. Each plan ends in a deployable shadow-safe state and must pass its own verification before the next begins.

1. `2026-09-09-anticipatory-market-foundation.md` — connect squeeze, structure, liquidity and derivatives evidence; persist opportunity state without affecting execution.
2. `2026-09-09-ai-thesis-execution-flow.md` — make AI create structured theses, add a critic, validate geometry, and route approved theses into deterministic Risk in shadow/DEMO.
3. `2026-09-09-trade-lifecycle-evaluation.md` — replay real trade lifecycles, measure cohort lift and implement evidence-based promotion gates.

LIVE activation, exchange credential creation and strategy approval remain separate operator actions after all three plans pass their acceptance gates.
